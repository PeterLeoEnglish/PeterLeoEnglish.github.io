About your teacher

Meet Peter Leo

Writer. Business owner. English teacher.

Practical experience and a lifelong interest in language are at the heart of Peter's teaching.

Peter Leo is a native British English speaker with over 35 years of experience as a professional writer. He has also owned and managed several businesses, giving him a wealth of practical experience in the world of business and communication.

For the past six years, Peter has been teaching English to intermediate and advanced learners from around the world, helping them improve their fluency, expand their vocabulary and develop greater confidence in using English.

His interests extend well beyond business English — from everyday conversation and current affairs to literature and storytelling.
